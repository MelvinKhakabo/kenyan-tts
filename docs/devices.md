## How to fill in the TBCs (Samsung)

- **Android version:** Settings → About phone → Software information → Android version
- **TalkBack version:** Settings → Accessibility → Installed apps or TalkBack → Settings → About TalkBack
- **Google TTS:** Settings → General management → Text-to-speech. Is "Speech Recognition & Synthesis from Google" listed as an engine?
- **Kiswahili voice:** in the Google engine's settings → Install voice data → is Kiswahili downloaded?

## Notes

- The Galaxy A13 is an entry-level phone, so the engine's speed must be tested on it, not only on the A16. A delay that feels fine on the A16 may be noticeable on the A13.
